Don’t predict the hit. Make a smarter next move.

An independent concept showing how a music marketing team might compare early listener signals across markets, see how the chosen priority changes the answer, and pick a controlled follow-up test before committing a larger budget.

Explore the simulator How it works

Independent concept developed for ENKO — not affiliated with or endorsed by ENKO. All releases, artists and figures are fictional.

The idea

One release. Multiple markets. One clearer next decision.

Completion, replay, stated intent and campaign cost can point in different directions. Which market is “best” depends on what you are trying to learn — so the decision should name its goal and show its reasoning.

  1. 01

    Name the priority

    Listener resonance, save intent or cost efficiency. The definition of “best market” depends on it.

  2. 02

    Compare the signals

    Completion, voluntary replay, stated save intent and cost per qualified action — side by side for each market.

  3. 03

    Choose the next test

    A transparent ranking points to a larger, controlled experiment — not to a full launch.

Step 02

How a real test would work

This is the research workflow the demo stands in for. Here, the “analysis” only reads preloaded fictional values.

  1. 1

    Recruit comparable listeners

    People in each selected market who already enjoy the release’s genre.

  2. 2

    Observe listening signals

    Measure listening completion and voluntary replay, ask about intent to save, and track the cost of each qualified action.

    • Completion %
    • Voluntary replay %
    • Stated save intent %
    • Cost per qualified action, USD
  3. 3

    Compare market indicators

    Rank the markets against one stated priority, then design a larger controlled test.

          Why the result changes.

          Listener resonance

          0.65 × completion % + 0.35 × voluntary replay %

          ↑ Higher ranks first

          Demonstration-only weights. Not a validated score or forecast.

          Save intent

          stated save intent %

          ↑ Higher ranks first

          A stated response, not an actual streaming-platform save. Intention does not guarantee behaviour.

          Cost efficiency

          cost per qualified action, USD

          ↓ Lower ranks first

          Illustrative values. Real campaigns would need quality checks and comparison controls.

          What this demo is not

          • Not a hit-prediction model, and not AI analysis.
          • Not a live study — no listeners, audio, ads, platforms or external data.
          • Not evidence about Poland, Germany or the United States as real markets.
          • Not a launch recommendation — at most, a direction for a larger controlled test.

          Open questions before any real pilot

          • Does a similar workflow already exist inside the team?
          • Is market selection the decision worth supporting?
          • Which data sources and permissions would be available?
          • Would sample sizes support a general conclusion? Roughly 50 synthetic profiles per market would not.

          A hypothesis to discuss — not a diagnosis.
